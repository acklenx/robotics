    Put programs in the remote's slots
    
  
    
    
    For a robot running ir.ino (the Remote page). Tabs 1 to 9 are the remote's slots 1 to 9. Slot programs are plain steps:
      f b l r and the diagonals q e in centimetres, t in degrees, p a pause in seconds,
      n "do it all again" so many times. Loops and subroutines are unrolled into those steps (waits are kept).
    
    
    This tab: 
    
    
    
      
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
      
      
    
    
    
    
    
    All nine slots at once
    
    
    
    
      
      
      
    
    
    
    
    
    What is on the robot now
    
    
    Read the nine slots back from a connected ir.ino robot. Each one can open as blocks: to look at, simulate, change and send back. A slot nobody has saved to shows its built-in demo.
    
    
    
      
      
      
    
    
    
      
    
      Paste the text into the console on the Remote page (or any serial console) and press Send. Then press a slot's number on the remote, or send p3; for slot 3.
