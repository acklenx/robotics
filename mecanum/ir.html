Numbers are how far (centimetres) or how much to turn (degrees), and which slot a program lives in.

1. Drive

Hold an arrow and the robot goes that way. Let go and it stops. ▲ forward, ▼ back, ◀ ▶ slide sideways (mecanum wheels!).

Hold 1 or 3 to turn left or right on the spot.

0, OK or # stop it.

If something is closer than 25 cm in front, the robot will not drive forward (the sonar sees it). Back up, slide or turn instead.

2. Make a program

A. Switch to Program Mode: *** the robot wiggles = "I am listening"

B. Add a step: * then a direction then a number. The first key after the * is always the move; the next * ends the number. The robot nods = "got it".

C. Add another step: * and the next move. Any of these:

  • ▲ ▼ ◀ ▶ + cm: forward, back, slide left, slide right
  • 1 3 + degrees: turn left, turn right
  • 7 9 + cm: the forward diagonals, left and right
  • 5 + seconds: pause
  • 0 + cm across: a circle, nose pointed at the middle
  • 8 + times: do everything before it again, that many times in all

D. Done: *** the robot wiggles = "which slot?"

E. Save it: a slot number 1..9 then ** the robot nods = saved.

No number? *▲ is 25 cm, *3 is a 90 degree turn, *5 is 1 second, *8 is 2 times, *0 is 1 m across.
Oops: # throws the program away (slow head shake). **** and a slot number adds to the program saved there instead of starting fresh.
Example: out, turn, back a bit, saved in slot 2
***wiggle = listening
*▲50forward 50 cm
*390turn right 90 degrees
*▼20back 20 cm
***wiggle = which slot?
2**nod = saved in slot 2
After each step the robot nods = "got it".

3. Run a program

* then the slot number: *3 runs the program in slot 3.

Any key stops it. An empty slot gets a slow head shake.

Up to 16 steps in a program, 9 slots. Programs stay on the robot when it is switched off.

*0 is always program 0: a circle 1 m across, facing in.

Out of the box the slots hold nine demos: 1 square, 2 crab box (slides only), 3 out and back, 4 triangle, 5 stairs, 6 spiral, 7 look around, 8 figure 8, 9 dance. Saving your own program in a slot replaces its demo.

Tips

Point the remote at the robot's receiver (the little black eye). Press keys one at a time. Wait for the nod before the next step.

Pressed * by mistake? Nothing happens if you wait 3 seconds. # always gets you out.

Forward 30 cm and turn right, four times, saved in slot 1. Run it with *1. Does it end where it started? If not, the robot needs calibrating.

Three steps instead of eight. No number after ▲ means 25 cm, none after 3 means 90 degrees, and 84 repeats everything before it until it has gone round 4 times in all: a 25 cm square.

Instructor

Set up a robot

Flash ir.ino (Arduino IDE, Uno). It drives on the same motion numbers as mecanum_holonomic.ino, including a set saved by the Calibrate / Tune page.

IR receiver (3 pins: OUT, VCC, GND): OUT to D10, the signal pin of the shield's SER1 servo header, 5 V and GND beside it. No soldering. Any other pin works too: change IR_RECEIVER_PIN at the top of the sketch (the robot says which pin it uses when it boots).

Sonar (HC-SR04) stays where the kit puts it: trig A3, echo A2. Under 25 cm ahead = stop. SONAR_STOP_MM sets the distance; SONAR_STOPS_EVERYTHING makes it stop every move, not only forward ones.

The remote is the kit's 17-key one (arrows, OK, 0-9, * #). Another remote prints ir: key 0x.. not mapped in the console below; its codes go in IR_KEYS.

Calibrate from the remote: ###

Three quick steps to get programmed moves in the ballpark. A test only runs when you press OK, so put the robot on a mark first; * runs the current test again whenever you want to re-measure. You type what you measured. The numbers are saved on the robot (the same place the Calibrate / Tune page saves), on top of the code's numbers. Strafing is left alone. You need a tape measure and a metre of floor.

Robot messages

If it does not work

Nothing happens on any keyIs ir.ino on the robot (connect: the status says ir)? Is the receiver's OUT on the pin the robot names at boot? Try the keys from this page: if those work, it is the receiver or the remote's battery.
Some keys are missedPoint straight at the eye; sunlight and some LED lights blind receivers. Press keys one at a time and wait for the nod.
It will not go forwardSomething is within 25 cm of the sonar, or the sonar is unplugged and reads 0. Check with Sonar reading.
Distances or turns are offIt drives on the calibration numbers: Calibrate / Tune, then try the square again.
Too wobbly when it nods / wigglesGESTURE_PWM_OVER_START and GESTURE_TWITCH_MS in the sketch.
