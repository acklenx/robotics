The light bot

Two light sensors on the robot's front corners let it see a flashlight. It can chase the light like a moth, or run from it like a cockroach. Everything starts from the remote.

Moth mode

##1

The robot nods, then waits. Shine a flashlight at its nose and walk away: it turns toward the brighter side and comes after you. Take the light away and it stops.

No remote? Flash the flashlight on the left sensor, the right, the left, the right, all within 3 seconds. It nods and goes into moth mode.

Cockroach mode

##2

Shine the flashlight at it and it backs away, turning so it keeps watching the light. Take the light away and it stops.

Any key stops either mode.

A light sensor on the back

Add a third sensor in the middle of the back, looking straight back, and light from behind works too:

  • Moth mode backs straight toward the light.
  • Cockroach mode runs forward, away from it.
  • Aim the flashlight so a little light also reaches one front sensor, and it turns its tail toward the light as it goes.

The robot checks for the back sensor every time a light mode starts. Without one it works just as before.

Wiring the sensors

Each sensor is a light-dependent resistor (LDR) plus a fixed resistor: +5V to LDR to the pin, and the resistor from the pin to Gnd. More light makes the reading go up.

SensorPinWhere
LeftA4Left front corner, looking 45 degrees out
RightA5Right front corner, looking 45 degrees out
Back (optional)A3Middle of the back, looking straight back. Unplug the sonar's Trig wire from A3 first.

Use the analog header's A0-5 row for the signal. The +5V and Gnd rows beside it are only power. Our class kit: GL5537 LDRs with 4.7k resistors. The diagram and the resistor for other LDRs are on the Wiring page.

Step by step: the right sensor (A5)

Three 20 cm female-female dupont wires. Your colors will be mixed up; in the pictures 1 is red (5V), 2 is black (Gnd) and 3 is blue (A5).

The back sensor is optional: the robot works with just the left and right ones.

If it does not work

It does thisTry this
Nods, then ignores the flashlightCheck the sensors with b from the Programming page's console. All zeros (B,0,0,0,0) means a sensor's top leg is on Gnd instead of +5V, or its wire is in the wrong row.
Goes the wrong wayLeft and right are swapped: swap the A4 and A5 wires.
Needs a brighter and brighter flashlightFlashlights dim as they warm up and their batteries run down. A room full of flashlights also raises what the robot thinks of as room light. Turn the other flashlights away, or start the mode again.
Runs into youNothing stops it but you: turn the flashlight off, or press any key.
