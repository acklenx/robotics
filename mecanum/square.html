Program a square

With the IR remote, one key at a time. ir.ino on the robot.
While you program, the robot does not move. It only wiggles or nods to say it got your keys. The pictures show what it will do when you run the program in step 12.

Point and go slow. Aim the remote at the robot's black eye. Press one key at a time and wait for the wiggle or nod.
Made a mistake? Press # to cancel, then start again at step 1.
Not quite a square? Every robot is a little different. Calibrate it: see the Remote guide.
