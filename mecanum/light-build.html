How to build a light sensor

LDR voltage divider circuit

What you need, for each sensor

  • 3 female-female dupont wires, 20 cm long
  • 1 LDR (light-dependent resistor, GL5537)
  • 1 resistor, 4.7k
  • Tape

The circuit: +5V to the LDR to A5, and the resistor from A5 to Gnd. More light lowers the LDR's resistance, so the reading on A5 goes up. When you are done, see How to use the Light Bot.

Step by step: the right sensor (A5)

Three 20 cm female-female dupont wires. Any colors will do: what matters is where each one goes. In the pictures, wire 1 is +5V, wire 2 is Gnd and wire 3 is A5.
